For families and friends

For the people who send the books

A page that makes somebody smile can be a lovely gift. But sending books into a correctional facility requires more checking than an ordinary parcel.

Before you order

  1. Check the exact receiving institution and its current incoming-publications policy.
  2. Confirm whether paperback copies are permitted and whether they must be new.
  3. Ask whether the book must ship directly from a publisher, bookstore or approved vendor.
  4. Confirm any approved-seller, portal, quantity, content and address requirements.
  5. Only then choose a seller and place an order. Retain the receipt and tracking information.

There is no nationwide guarantee that any facility will accept a particular book. Rules and individual circumstances vary.
