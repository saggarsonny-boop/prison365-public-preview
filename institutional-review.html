For libraries, educators and book programs

Prison 365 is an illustrated daily activity annual. Review the actual contents, request a copy for evaluation, and decide whether it suits your readers and your institution.

What the 2027 edition contains

  • 365 dated daily readings and journal spaces
  • Cartoons and short, self-contained humor
  • 52 weekly puzzles with an answer key
  • Drawing, wordplay, short-writing and conversation prompts
  • Religious, cultural and civil observances with qualified calendar notes
  • Optional secular and faith-oriented reflection paths

Before any institutional endorsement or order

  1. Read the complete proof, not just the promotional excerpts.
  2. Assess whether illustration and text contrast survive your intended print format.
  3. Confirm institutional restrictions and the available purchasing channel.
  4. Review any cultural, religious or safety concerns in your setting.
  5. Request current price, edition information, ISBN and fulfillment terms once final.

No claim is made that any prison system, library or nonprofit has approved, purchased or endorsed this book. Bulk and sponsored-copy pricing are not yet available.
