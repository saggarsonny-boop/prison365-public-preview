Preparing to self-surrender

A book for the day you have, not a life plan

If you are approaching incarceration, there may be a great deal of uncertainty. This book is a daily companion with puzzles, cartoons and things to think about—not legal or institutional instructions.

What this book can offer

  • A brief page to open when you want something ordinary and interesting
  • Small puzzles and humor without login, batteries or an internet connection
  • Optional prompts that do not demand private disclosures or forced positivity

What it cannot promise

Prison 365 is not a guide to surrender procedures, a substitute for counsel, a mental-health treatment, or a guarantee that a facility will accept the paperback. Ask your lawyer and the receiving institution for authoritative instructions specific to your situation.
